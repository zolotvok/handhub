    Calibration

    Calibrate cameras to each other

    Walk slowly around the play area for 30 seconds and pause for a moment in several places, arms a little away from your body. Your whole body is used, so keep your feet in view of the side cameras.

    After you press Start there is a 3 second countdown so you can get into position. The same control is under the previews on the Cameras tab.

    Headset camera calibration

    Nothing to press. Wear the headset, keep one hand where the headset camera and at least two other cameras can see it, and look around a little.

    The hub learns where the headset camera sits by itself and keeps it up to date while it runs. Looking around makes that faster. Press “Learn again” only if the phone was put back into the headset differently. This needs the headset camera to be on and a VR app to be running. The same status is under the previews on the Cameras tab while the headset camera is on.

    Standing calibration

    Optional. With your height the 3D size of the room is corrected too. Leave empty if you do not know it.

    Stand upright in the play area, facing the laptop, where two cameras can see you from head to foot.

    Sets the camera tilt from your upright body. After you press Start there is a 3 second countdown so you can get into position.

    Anchor hands to head

    Hold both hands beside your head, palms toward the camera, thumb tips touching your temples.

    Use this when the hands appear in the wrong place, for example behind you. The pose is taken after a 3 second countdown. In the playground, the C key does the same.

    Set rest pose

    Hold both hands in front of your chest.

    Press the button, then get into position. The pose is taken after a 3 second countdown.

    Align headset

    Look straight at the camera.

    Then press the button. This needs the headset to be connected.
